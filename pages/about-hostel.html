About Our Hostel

Vagdevi Vidyarthini Nilaya

Kunchitigara Sarvajanika Seva Datti (R.) - Vagdevi Vidyarthini Nilaya was established to support girls, especially those from rural and economically weaker families, in pursuing higher education.

The organization was founded in 2001 under the Indian Trust Act with the motto: "There is nothing as sacred as knowledge in this world."

Our Journey

2001 - Organization Founded

Kunchitigara Sarvajanika Seva Datti (R.) was registered in 2001 under the Indian Trust Act to support education and students.

Land Donation

Mr. B.R. Rajeev donated 10 guntas of land in Hegganahalli, Bengaluru, for establishing a girls' hostel.

Early Support for Students

Before the hostel building was constructed, Smt. Jyothi Balakrishna and other donors provided free accommodation for students in their homes for several years.

2008 - Hostel Building

A hostel building costing approximately ₹1.5 crore was constructed through donations and inaugurated in 2008.

2023 - Third Floor Expansion

Due to increasing demand, construction of an additional third floor began in October 2023 using trust funds.

Hostel Capacity

The hostel has 22 rooms and can accommodate approximately 80-85 students.

More than 2,000 students have benefited from the hostel so far, including students from the Kunchitigara community and other communities.

Facilities and Student Support

The hostel provides basic facilities and support to help students continue their education in Bengaluru.

  • Accommodation
  • Clean and healthy food
  • Drinking water
  • 24-hour hot water
  • Computer laboratory access
  • Support for higher education

The hostel operates on a non-profit basis, with students contributing towards actual expenses such as food, electricity, water and staff-related costs.

Third Floor Expansion

Due to the increasing demand for accommodation, the trust started construction of additional rooms on the third floor in 2023.

The additional rooms are intended to support girls pursuing medical studies, engineering, competitive examinations and other higher education.
