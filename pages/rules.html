Vidyarthini Nilaya (Girls' Hostel) Rules and Regulations

Students are expected to follow the following rules and regulations during their stay in the hostel.

  1. Students must properly utilize all facilities provided in the hostel during their stay.
  2. Students should conduct themselves well, respecting the sacrifices made by the hostel management and staff, and build a bright future using these facilities.
  3. Students must keep their rooms clean, sweep morning and evening, clean the walls and cobwebs monthly, and throw garbage into the designated dustbins. Sitting on tables is strictly prohibited.
  4. Parents, relatives, friends, or visitors are not allowed inside the hostel rooms. Designated visiting hours in external common areas must be used.
  5. Use of heavy electrical appliances like electric heaters, irons, stoves, computers, etc., in rooms is strictly prohibited. Students will be held personally responsible for any damage or mishaps caused.
  6. Students must use bath water, tap water, and borewell water carefully without wasting it.
  7. Students must treat fellow boarders like sisters, maintain friendship, and behave with respect, love, and affection toward the warden and cook.
  8. Any problems inside the hostel must be brought to the warden's or secretary's attention first. Indiscipline or disobedience will attract strict disciplinary action.
  9. Food must be consumed only in the dining hall. Take only required quantities, do not leave food scraps on tables, throw plates and waste in bins, clean table surfaces, and do not sit on tables to eat.
  10. If leaving the hostel, such as going home, out-of-station, college trips, or staying with relatives, prior written permission must be obtained from the Warden or Secretary.
  11. Hostel timings are from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM. If students wish to enter the hostel after 7:00 PM, they must obtain prior permission from the warden.
  12. Students must attend meals and prayers on time. Attendance is mandatory during evening joint prayers. Biometric finger registration is compulsory when entering or leaving the hostel.
  13. Furniture items such as cots, chairs, tables, fans, locks, windows, etc., must be used carefully. Upon leaving the hostel, items must be handed back in good condition. Students will bear repair costs if damaged.
  14. (A) The annual hostel academic period runs from August 1 to July 31. Existing students continuing studies must submit re-admission applications by July 25.
  15. (B) New applicants must submit filled application forms between June 1 and June 30.
  16. Students must focus on studies, perform well in all college exams, and submit mark cards immediately after each semester result. Failing an exam leads to loss of hostel accommodation without fee refunds.
  17. Students residing in the hostel are not permitted to take up any government or private employment. Doing so will lead to disciplinary action.
  18. Students must keep the hostel premises and restrooms clean, follow instructions from the warden and management diligently, and pay for any repairs caused by misuse.

Important

All students are expected to follow these rules carefully and cooperate with the hostel management to maintain a safe, clean and disciplined environment.
